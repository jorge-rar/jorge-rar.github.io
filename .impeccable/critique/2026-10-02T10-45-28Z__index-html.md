## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** The desktop layout follows the experience timeline: date in the left column, title and summary in the main column, tags beneath. “Study & Development Period” works as the entry title; a separate job title is not needed. On mobile, CSS moves the date below the title, followed by summary and tags. Keep these positions.

The four tags use 10px text, smaller than the 12px tool labels in the Physical entry, making them hard to scan and inconsistent.

**Deterministic scan:** The detector flags Python, SQL, GitHub and HTML as functional text at 10px, below its 11px minimum. A general clipped-overflow warning also appeared, with no clear evidence of impact on this entry.

**Visual inspection:** Desktop preview shows the date at left and title, paragraph and four outlined tags to the right. The tags fit in a single row but are visibly small. Mobile order was confirmed from CSS; no mobile screenshot was available. No overlay was injected.

## Overall Impression

The title and date hierarchy works and matches the other entries. The tag size is the one clear presentation issue; raising it to 12px would improve legibility and align it with Physical.

## What's Working

- The title clearly names the period without implying an employer or job role.
