## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Sentence fragment.** “Ensuring…” is not a complete sentence after the preceding full stop. Join or rewrite the thought.
2. **[P2] Generic, unsupported descriptor.** “One of Portugal’s fastest-growing gym chains” is promotional and time-sensitive. Remove it unless sourced and necessary.
3. **[P2] Repeated responsibilities.** Planning, coordinating and supervising in the paragraph overlaps with several bullets. Keep the paragraph to role scope and context.
4. **[P2] Dense, non-semantic bullet formatting.** Six hyphen-led lines inside a paragraph take longer to scan and are not announced as list items by assistive technology. Use a real list and combine overlapping progress, quality and handover points where appropriate.

Suggested overview: “Planned and delivered construction and maintenance projects across multiple gym locations, aligning design, budget and execution.”

## Persona Red Flags

- **Recruiter scanning quickly:** the core scope is buried in a long introductory sentence.
- **Construction project manager:** budget and opening-date constraints are present in the bullets but not prominent.
- **Screen-reader user:** line breaks and hyphens inside a paragraph do not provide list semantics.

## Minor Observations

“Selected contractors and suppliers according to project requirements, availability and constraints” can be sharpened with specific criteria only where those criteria reflect the work actually performed.

## Questions to Consider
