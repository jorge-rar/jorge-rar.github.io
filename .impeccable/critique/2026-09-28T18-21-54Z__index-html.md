## Design Specificity Verdict

The portrait in an orange engineering orbit, manufacturing-focused language, coordinates, and restrained palette make the portfolio recognizable as Jorge’s. The name/rotating-role/CTA composition and boxed About attributes remain familiar portfolio patterns. A stronger through-line around work in motion or process improvement could connect the two sections. This is a design judgment, not a detector result.

## Overall Impression

The Hero establishes a person and an industrial context quickly. The main opportunity is to make its concise first impression lead into About rather than repeat it; the animated copy and dense topic groups add effort to an otherwise strong structure.

## Cognitive Load

Three checklist failures: single focus (rotating title and three actions compete); chunking (ten interests and five soft skills exceed four items per group); minimal choices (six top-level navigation links). Grouping, general visual hierarchy, sequential reading, no cross-screen memory bridge, and progressive disclosure of CV formats pass. Overall cognitive load: moderate.

## What's Working

1. Portrait and orbital geometry give the Hero a personal, industrial visual signature.
2. Named tools and scope make the profile more credible than generic claims.
3. About expands into languages and personal interests, revealing more of the person.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Repeated, justified profile copy.** Hero and About repeat years, sectors, and capability areas; justification creates uneven word gaps. Fix: give Hero one concise positioning sentence, keep the expanded profile in About, and left-align both. Suggested `$impeccable distill` and `$impeccable typeset`.
2. **[P1] Typewriter accessibility and reading stability.** The parent and child both use `aria-live`, while the text updates character by character; this may create repeated screen-reader announcements and visible mid-word states. Fix: provide one stable accessible label and decide whether the visual role should rotate more slowly, pause, or be static. Suggested `$impeccable harden` and `$impeccable animate`.
3. **[P2] Dense About topic groups.** Ten interests, five soft skills, and three development topics compete at the same visual level and may become a long wrapping list on narrow screens. Fix: group interests by theme or prioritize a few while keeping the rest available. Suggested `$impeccable distill` and `$impeccable adapt`.
4. **[P2] Several early navigation choices.** The Hero offers Experience, Contact, and CV, while the top navigation exposes six sections. Fix: strengthen the primary action and group or demote secondary paths. Suggested `$impeccable clarify` and `$impeccable layout`.
