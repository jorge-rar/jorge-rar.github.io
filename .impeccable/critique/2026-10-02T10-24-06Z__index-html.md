## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** The desktop layout is effective: a 200px side rail separates the employment metadata from the main experience content. “Physical” is the dominant label, with the orange uppercase role immediately below. On mobile, the company and role precede the date, website and location, a sensible reading order. Preserve this structure.

The hierarchy can be made easier to scan by slightly increasing the role and metadata text sizes from 12px. Keep the company name most prominent and metadata secondary. The left column does not need repositioning; the date, website and location are already grouped.

## Overall Impression

The company-role-metadata arrangement is sound. A modest size increase for the role and metadata would improve legibility without changing the content or the established hierarchy.

## What's Working

- “Physical” has clear visual priority as the employer.
- The orange role line is distinct and appears directly below the employer.
- Date, website and location are grouped in a dedicated desktop column and follow the role on mobile.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P2] Metadata is small on desktop.** At 12px and muted gray, date, website and location are easy to overlook. Raise them slightly, for example to 13–14px, while retaining secondary contrast.
2. **[P2] The role is small for a key identifier.** The 12px uppercase line reads as a label. A modest increase to 13–14px would improve discoverability while keeping “Physical” dominant.
3. **[P3] All metadata lines have the same visual weight.** Their grouping is clear; subtle consistent line spacing can make each value easier to scan without changing order or content.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Recruiter scanning quickly:** may notice “Physical” but miss the 12px role or dates.
- **Mobile reader:** order is clear; metadata appears after the role as intended.

## Minor Observations

No need to move the metadata to the main column. Preserve the current desktop side rail and mobile order.

## Questions to Consider

- Should the role and metadata both become slightly larger, or should the current quiet hierarchy remain unchanged?
